DATA DICTIONARY

1. Customer_ID
- Unique identification number for each customer
- Data type: Integer
- Purpose: Helps to uniquely identify each record

2. Name
- Name of the customer
- Data type: String
- Purpose: Basic customer identification

3. DOB
- Date of Birth of customer
- Data type: Date
- Purpose: Used to calculate age

4. Gender
- Gender of customer (Male/Female)
- Data type: String
- Purpose: Customer segmentation

5. City
- City where customer belongs
- Data type: String
- Purpose: Location-based analysis

6. Purchase_Amount
- Amount spent by customer
- Data type: Float
- Purpose: Understand spending behavior

7. Purchase_Date
- Date of transaction
- Data type: Date
- Purpose: Time-based analysis

FEATURE ENGINEERING:

8. Customer_Age
- Calculated from DOB
- Purpose: Age-based insights

9. Spending_Category
- High or Low based on purchase amount
- Purpose: Customer classification
